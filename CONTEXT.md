# Domain glossary

- **GEO toolkit**: Reusable tools and agent instructions that help a product become discoverable and support evidence-based improvement.
- **Product fact**: A product statement supplied with a traceable source; the toolkit does not infer new capabilities.
- **Technical audit**: Checks of available website artifacts, with the scope and evidence recorded for every finding.
- **Finding**: A check result whose status is pass, fail, or unknown, with its evidence and proposed next action.
- **Content opportunity**: A proposed topic tied to a supplied product fact and a supplied audience question.
- **Visibility sample**: A recorded AI answer or an explicitly recorded collection error, with its query conditions and raw evidence.
- **Mention rate**: The proportion of successful samples containing an explicit configured brand name or alias.
- **Citation rate**: The proportion of successful samples containing a structured citation to an owned host.
- **Coverage**: Successful samples divided by all attempted samples, reported alongside visibility rates.
- **Core edition**: The toolkit edition that can be verified without paid AI services.
