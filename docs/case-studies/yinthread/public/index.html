Two birthdays, one reading. BaZi ("eight characters") is the classical Chinese birth chart, built from the date and hour you were born. We compare two charts and score how they fit.

What is BaZi?›

Four "pillars" — year, month, day, hour — each a stem over a branch, each carrying one of the five elements (the colours below). The day stem is the "day master"; the day branch the "spouse palace".
