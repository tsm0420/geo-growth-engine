# Direction research: evidence before expansion

Observed: 2026-10-18, with the observation clock read at 15:20:56 UTC. Scope: two public official documentation pages and the existing [core contract](../spec.md), at planning checkpoint `c9f85a564e437097abf1965adf622727d9aeec67`. No product measurement, account access, live product crawling, installation or implementation was performed. This report checks responsible promises; it does not establish demand.

## Verified external guidance

**Google.** Ordinary Search eligibility applies to AI Overviews and AI Mode: supporting pages need indexing and snippet eligibility, and meeting the requirements does not guarantee indexing or serving. Google specifies no additional AI text file or special structured-data requirement. Its guidance includes accessible text, internal links and crawler access, including hosting infrastructure. Traffic from these AI features is included in Search Console's overall Web performance reporting. Google also describes variation between AI features and their responses. Source: [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), especially technical requirements, measurement and content controls; retrieved 2026-10-18, page update shown as 2025-12-10.

**OpenAI.** OAI-SearchBot manages automatic Search crawling, while GPTBot concerns potential foundation-model training; their settings are independent. Search guidance includes both robots.txt permission and access from published IP ranges. ChatGPT-User represents certain user-triggered actions, is not an automatic crawler, and does not determine Search inclusion. OpenAI says robots.txt rules may not apply to those user-triggered actions. Source: [Overview of OpenAI Crawlers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), retrieved 2026-10-18; no publication/update date was displayed in the retrieved article text.

## Boundaries supported by the existing contract

The [core specification](../spec.md) separates a technical audit of local artifacts from deployed delivery, indexing and ranking. Visibility samples are imported records with collection conditions and retained raw evidence. Citation rate counts owned-host citations among successful samples; failed attempts affect coverage. These are repository definitions, not a certification by either platform. This research did not rerun or validate their implementation.

## Strategic implications — inference

The defensible initial promise is a reproducible review: identify problems in supplied artifacts, connect recommendations to product facts, and inspect recorded answers. A special AI-file checklist or a numerical promise of discoverability would overstate the cited guidance. Local permission checks should remain distinguishable from actual network access and observed inclusion.

Recorded answers should retain their exact provider, surface, model, locale, question and observation time. A manually copied consumer answer is evidence of that observation; it does not authenticate an API result or establish representative platform coverage. An observed citation-rate difference is descriptive. It does not establish that a website edit caused the difference, nor that sampled citation rates equal traffic share. These interpretations combine the local measurement contract with the platform distinctions above.

## Recommended next phase — proposal, not implementation authorization

Focus on a free first-use workflow for a website or SaaS maintainer who can supply an HTML export and product facts. Have one documented path from setup through a technical audit, an evidence-linked action list and a later comparison. Keep mobile workflows available, but validate the narrower path before adding another product surface.

Define a manual collection protocol before automating sampling: record conditions, raw answers or errors, sampling windows and missing context. Specify when two collections are comparable and when the result must remain unknown. Test future changes at the existing CLI seam, including mixed conditions and absent evidence.

Request an owner-supplied pilot packet and record setup friction, findings the owner can act on, evidence they cannot collect and whether they repeat the workflow. Publish only an explicitly approved case packet. A successful pilot would demonstrate usability and reproducibility; causal growth claims need a different study.

## Unknowns and investment gates

Paid providers, a hosted dashboard and broad crawler expansion should wait for pilot evidence identifying an unmet need and a separately accepted contract.
